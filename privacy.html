Privacy Policy

BluFlame AI · Last updated June 24, 2026

This privacy policy describes how BluFlame AI ("we," "us") handles information for our SMS account-balance service. The service lets a single, pre-authorized family member text our number to receive a prepaid card balance.

Information we receive

When you text our number we receive your mobile phone number and the contents of your message. We use this only to (1) confirm you are the authorized recipient and (2) reply with the requested account balance.

How we use it

Your phone number and messages are used solely to operate this balance-reply service. We do not use your information for marketing.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. We do not share your mobile information, phone number, or message contents with any third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be processed only by the messaging carrier strictly to deliver the text messages you request.

SMS consent & opt-out

You receive messages only because you personally requested this service. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for assistance. Message and data rates may apply.

Data retention

We retain only the minimal records needed to operate and de-duplicate messages, and remove them when no longer needed.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the account owner who set up this service for you.
